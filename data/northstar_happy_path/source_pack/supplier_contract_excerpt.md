# Master Supply Agreement — Excerpt
## Between NorthStar Robotics ("Buyer") and Pacific Motion Systems ("Supplier")
### Sections 7 and 9 — Delivery, Late Delivery, and Expedite

**7.1 Delivery terms.** All deliveries are DAP Buyer's Fremont, California production facility (Incoterms 2020), on or before the delivery date stated in the applicable purchase order. Time of delivery is of the essence. Partial deliveries are permitted only with Buyer's prior written consent.

**7.2 Advance notice of delay.** Supplier shall notify Buyer in writing within two (2) business days of becoming aware of any anticipated delay to a confirmed delivery date, stating the cause of the delay and the revised delivery date.

**7.3 Late-delivery penalty.** If Supplier fails to deliver conforming goods by the confirmed delivery date, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the total value of the affected purchase order for each week (or part thereof) of delay, capped at 5% of the purchase order value. Accrual of liquidated damages does not limit Buyer's right to cover under Section 9.2.

**9.1 Expedite requests.** Buyer may at any time request expedited production or expedited shipment of a confirmed order. Supplier shall respond within two (2) business days, stating feasibility, the earliest achievable delivery date, and any expedite premium.

**9.2 Right to cover.** If Supplier fails to deliver by the confirmed date and Buyer reasonably procures substitute goods from an alternate source, Supplier shall be liable for the price difference between the contract price and the cover price for the quantities not delivered on time, in addition to liquidated damages under Section 7.3, subject to the cap therein.
